Klick — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026

한국어로 보기 ↓

Klick is a metronome app for Android and Wear OS, made by an individual developer (sangwonkimm).

What we collect

Nothing. Klick does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app does not request internet access.

Data that stays on your device

Your settings (tempo, time signature, sounds, display options) and your setlist are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

If you use the Wear OS watch app, playback state and your setlist are sent directly between your own phone and watch through Google Play services (Wearable Data Layer). This data is not sent to the developer.

기기에만 저장되는 정보

설정(템포, 박자표, 소리, 화면 옵션)과 세트리스트는 사용자의 기기에만 저장됩니다. 앱을 삭제하거나 앱 데이터를 지우면 함께 삭제됩니다.

Wear OS 워치 앱을 쓰면 재생 상태와 세트리스트가 Google Play 서비스(Wearable Data Layer)를 통해 사용자 본인의 폰과 워치 사이에서만 오갑니다. 개발자에게 전송되지 않습니다.
