Withday — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026

한국어로 보기 ↓

Withday is a shared calendar, to-do and D-day app for families on Android, made by an individual developer (sangwonkimm).

What we collect

DataWhy, and where it goes
Google account email and display nameSign-in, and to show who is in your family space. Stored in Supabase (our server).
Events, to-dos, to-do lists and D-days you create; family members' names and colorsThe core of the app: sharing them with your family. Stored in Supabase and on your device.
Device calendar events, only for calendars you choose to shareShow them to your family. You choose per calendar to share nothing, only "busy", or the full details. With "busy", the title is not uploaded. Stored in Supabase only if you turn sharing on.
Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)Tell your family's devices that something changed. Stored in Supabase; sent to Google's FCM to deliver notifications.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell or share data for marketing.
  • Only members of the same family space can read that space's data. Access is enforced on the server (row-level security).
  • Data is sent over HTTPS.

Data on your device

The app keeps a local copy of your family's data so it works offline. Device calendars that you have not turned on for sharing are only read on your device to show them to you, and are not uploaded.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
InternetSync with your family.
Read calendarShow your device calendars in Withday, and share the ones you choose.
NotificationsEvent reminders and family activity alerts.
Exact alarmsDeliver event reminders on time.
Run at startupReschedule reminders after the phone restarts.

Third parties

  • Supabase stores the data above.
  • Google (Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging) handles sign-in and notification delivery.

Deleting your data

Email the address below to delete your account and its data. Uninstalling the app removes the local copy on that device only.

Children

Withday is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Children in a family are added by an adult as names and colors only, without accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
